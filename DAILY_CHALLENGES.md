# Daily Challenges

Unique challenges each day with bonus rewards and streak tracking.

## Features

- **Daily Challenge**: New challenge every day
- **Special Conditions**: Max moves, timer, no hints, perfect accuracy, combo streak
- **Bonus Rewards**: 500-3000+ bonus points for meeting conditions
- **Streak Tracking**: Consecutive day completion
- **Achievements**: Special daily challenge achievements

## Challenge Types

- **Max Moves** - Complete in X moves or fewer
- **Timer** - Complete within X seconds
- **No Hints** - Complete without hints
- **Perfect Accuracy** - Maintain X% accuracy
- **Combo Streak** - Achieve X combo streak

## Streak Milestones

- 3 days - Small bonus
- 7 days - Week Warrior achievement
- 14 days - Bonus rewards
- 30 days - Monthly Master achievement

## Rewards

- Base completion points
- Condition bonus (500-3000+ points)
- Difficulty multipliers
- +150 XP bonus
- Streak bonuses
