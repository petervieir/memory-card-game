# Difficulty System

6 progressive difficulty levels with unlocking system.

## Levels

| Level | Cards | Trios | Grid | Multiplier | Unlock |
|-------|-------|-------|------|------------|--------|
| Beginner | 18 | 6 | 3×6 | 1.0x | Default |
| Intermediate | 24 | 8 | 4×6 | 1.2x | Complete Beginner |
| Advanced | 30 | 10 | 5×6 | 1.5x | Complete Intermediate |
| Expert | 42 | 14 | 6×7 | 1.8x | Complete Advanced |
| Legend | 60 | 20 | 8×8 | 2.2x | Complete Expert |
| Master | 96 | 32 | 8×12 | 2.5x | Complete Legend |

## Unlocking

- Complete a level to unlock the next
- Visual indicators: 🔒 locked, ✅ completed
- Per-wallet progress tracking
- Unlock notifications

## Scoring

- Base points increase with difficulty
- Multipliers scale from 1.0x to 2.5x
- Higher difficulties = higher score potential
