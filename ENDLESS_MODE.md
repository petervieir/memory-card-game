# Endless Mode

Progressive difficulty mode with limited lives.

## How It Works

- **Start**: Begin at Beginner difficulty
- **Progression**: Complete levels to advance automatically
- **Lives**: 3 lives total - each mistake costs 1 life
- **Scoring**: Points accumulate across all levels
- **Victory**: Complete all 6 difficulties
- **Defeat**: Lose all 3 lives

## Features

- Progressive difficulty (Beginner → Master)
- Lives system (3 lives)
- Cumulative scoring
- Leaderboard tracking
- Personal best tracking

## Achievements

- **Endless Explorer** - Complete first run
- **Endless Survivor** - Reach level 3
- **Endless Warrior** - Reach level 5
- **Endless Champion** - Complete all 6 levels
- **Endless High Scorer** - Score 1000+ points
- **Endless Legend** - Score 2500+ points
- **Clutch Player** - Complete level with 1 life
- **Perfect Endless Level** - Complete without losing lives
